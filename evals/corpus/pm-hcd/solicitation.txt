.................................................................................................. 12 Page 3 of 13 1 Background USDA’s Office of the Chief Information Officer (OCIO) seeks senior product management and human-centered design expertise to help inform and drive priority application development and modernization efforts across the Department. These efforts are aligned to high priority Departmental initiatives, including but not limited to the Farmer’s First Policy Agenda, and the National Farm Security Action Plan. A primary vision of USDA OCIO is to streamline service delivery to the public through well-defined, automated, and repeatable enterprise service offerings that can be leveraged across the Department to meet multiple program delivery needs. Initial areas of focus are Farm Production and Conservation (FPAC) Application Modernization efforts and prototypes within the Palantir environment to create data-centric, repeatable, and scalable service delivery across the entire portfolio of FPAC products and services. Some of these efforts could be applicable and scalable to other USDA mission areas. To determine the best approach and strategies to meet the OCIO mission, this effort must include key senior industry expertise in product management and human-centered design to ensure our efforts create the best products that fit the needs of our many different stakeholders across USDA programs. 2 Scope The scope of this requirement includes provision of the following capabilities: • Senior Product Management to lead product governance, roadmap development, and integration of Palantir ontology-driven workflows, ensuring alignment with OCIO objectives and compliance standards. • Human-Centered Design Expertise to conduct user research, develop service design artifacts, and ensure accessibility compliance (Section 508). The contractor must support agile delivery, produce documented deliverables, and operate within USDA security and governance frameworks. 3 Requirements The requirements outlined below represent the activities the contractor will perform. 3.1 Product Management The contractor must provide senior level product management expertise for FPAC’s system modernization and development efforts focusing on but not limited to Acreage Reporting, Farm Program Pilot, NRCS Integrated Field tools (IFT), NRCS Water and Climate Information System (WCIS), One Farmer/One file and Agriculture Foreign Investment Disclosure Act (AFIDA). Product Management includes: Page 4 of 13 • Platform vision statement and roadmap based on FPAC business needs • Platform branding, communications, and value proposition statement to align USDA IT and FPAC business leadership and ensure common objectives of administration, Secretary, and USDA strategic objectives • Scheduled (bi-weekly) and ad hoc communications support to all FPAC business leadership from OCIO, Platform team, and project team to ensure validity of use cases • Work with stakeholders to validate use cases, prioritize features and ensure stakeholder alignment to the vision and roadmap Expertise will be leveraged to help determine applicability and viability of FPAC development efforts on other USDA mission areas and staff offices. 3.2 Human Centered Design The contractor must provide senior level human centered design (HCD) expertise. HCD includes: • Lead Comprehensive User Research: Plan and execute qualitative and quantitative research (interviews, surveys, usability testing) to deeply understand stakeholder needs across FPAC programs, ensuring insights drive design decisions. • Develop Service Design Artifacts: Create and maintain user journey maps, service blueprints, personas, and workflow diagrams that align with Palantir ontology-driven workflows and USDA modernization objectives. • Accessibility and Compliance Expertise: Ensure all designs meet Section 508 and WCAG standards, embedding accessibility principles into prototypes and final deliverables for inclusive user experiences. • Facilitate Co-Creation and Stakeholder Alignment: Lead collaborative design workshops and ideation sessions with cross-functional teams (OCIO, FPAC business units, Palantir platform teams) to validate concepts and prioritize features. • Drive Iterative Design in Agile Delivery: Champion rapid prototyping, usability testing, and continuous feedback loops within an agile framework to deliver user-centered solutions that adapt to evolving program goals. 4 Deliverables Table 1: Deliverables Deliverable
